# Activities

**Activities** is a second way to look at a day's plan: the same content as the classic day sidebar,
presented as a draggable list of activity cards rather than a timeline.

You reach it from the planner's day panel, which has a two-way switch between the classic view and
the activity view. Nothing is stored differently — the switch changes presentation, not data.

## What an activity is

An activity is one item on a day: a place you plan to visit, a note, or a booking that occupies that
day. Creating one from the activity view creates the same underlying record the classic view would.

Each activity carries:

- A **title** — the place name, note text or booking title
- An optional **time**, which is what orders it within the day
- An optional **icon**, for notes
- **Links to a booking**, when the activity is a reservation

## Why two views

The classic day sidebar is a dense timeline optimised for sequencing a day — reordering, seeing
travel time between stops, spotting gaps. The activity view is a lighter, card-based list optimised
for skimming and for quick creation.

Both are legitimate ways to work, and people differ. The switch remembers nothing: reopening the
planner gives you the classic view, so a one-off look at the activity list does not change how the
planner behaves next time.

## Dragging between days

Activities can be dragged, including **across days** in the planner — dropping one on another day
moves the underlying assignment, exactly as dragging a place in the classic view does.

## Reordering and time

An activity with a time is ordered by it. Reordering a timed activity is restricted the same way it
is in the classic view: Tourism-Team will refuse an edit that breaks the chronological order of
timed items and bookings, and says so, rather than silently producing a day whose times no longer
make sense.

Remove the time first if you want to sort that item freely.
